Panel Check

Test patterns for a new display. Run the full sweep, or jump to one pattern. Patterns are drawn at native device pixels, so the fine grids are real 1 px lines rather than scaled ones. Best in fullscreen, on a clean screen (dust reads as a dead pixel), after the panel has been on for 20 minutes.

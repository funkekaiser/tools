Merge and split PDFs

Choose one or more PDFs, then say which pages you want, in which order.

What should come out?

Leave the pages empty for all of them, or type them like 1-3, 7. Start with not to leave pages out.

    Pages are copied exactly as they are, not redrawn, so text stays sharp and searchable and pictures lose nothing. Bookmarks, fill-in forms and the document's properties, such as its author and title, are left behind. A PDF that is locked against changes cannot be taken apart: print it to a new PDF and use that copy.

    Keep your own copy of this page — or press ⌘ S.

    Everything happens on your device. Nothing you type is sent or saved anywhere.

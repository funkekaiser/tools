Each pick takes 53 random bits from your browser's cryptographic generator, the same source the password maker uses. The quick way to turn those bits into a number from 1 to n is to take the remainder after dividing by n, but unless n divides 253 exactly, that makes the low numbers very slightly more likely. Here the few values at the top that would cause the lean are thrown away and drawn again, so every outcome has exactly the same chance. "Thrown away" counts how often that has happened on this page. Names kept out of the hat are drawn by a Fisher–Yates shuffle, so every order is equally likely.

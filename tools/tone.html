Tone generator

Type a pitch, or two with a dash for a sweep, or choose noise, then press Play. Start quiet and turn it up slowly.

What is playing

Hz

Space plays and stops. ← → move the pitch by 1 Hz, with Shift by 0.1 Hz.

No sound? Turn off silent mode, then press Play again.

Sound comes from your browser's Web Audio engine: an oscillator for tones and sweeps, and eight seconds of generated noise on a loop. Every start, stop and change glides over about 30 milliseconds, so nothing clicks. "Evenly by ear" is a logarithmic sweep and "evenly by Hz" a linear one. Pink noise is white noise filtered to fall by 3 dB each octave, so every octave carries the same power. Level is in dB below full scale, peak for waves and average for noise; the volume slider is squared, which is closer to how loudness is heard. Square and saw waves are played at half and 60% level. Wavelength is in air at 20 °C. The settings live after the # in the address, which a browser never sends anywhere.
